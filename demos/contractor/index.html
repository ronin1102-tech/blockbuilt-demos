03 — What it costs

A planning band, before you call anyone.

Two questions, no form. This is what these jobs run in Cleveland — not what yours will cost, and not a quote.

Question 1 of 2

What moves the number

  • Structure. A wall that turns out to be load-bearing, or a floor that has to come up.
  • Mechanicals. Old knob-and-tube, cast iron, or a panel that has to be replaced.
  • Finish. Custom cabinetry and stone move a kitchen more than any other line.
  • Surprises. We open walls in the first week, on purpose, so we find them early.

Bands are for a typical 1920s–1950s Cleveland house, before allowances, and exclude design fees and permits.

04 — How it goes

What happens after you call.

The most common thing homeowners tell us they couldn’t find out from anyone else’s site.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough

    Sixty to ninety minutes on site. We look at the structure, the panel, the plumbing and what you actually want. No charge, no pressure, and we’ll say if it isn’t a job for us.

    Free · within a week of your call

  2. 02

    Written proposal

    Itemized, emailed as a PDF, every line priced. Allowance lines where you’ll be picking finishes, so nothing gets discovered later as a change order.

    7–10 business days after the walkthrough

  3. 03

    Build

    A start date in writing before anything is ordered. The same crew for the duration, a photo every Friday, and a call from Daniel each week.

    Dust barriers up, floors covered, one bathroom always working

  4. 04

    Punch list

    We walk the job together and fix what’s on the list. Then we come back at one year for the things that settle, shift or stick.

    One-year callback, in writing
